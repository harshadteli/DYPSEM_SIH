Judging Framework

How Ideas Are Evaluated

A panel of industry experts and academic mentors rigorously assess every submitted idea across 9 key criteria. Understand what makes a winning pitch stand out.

Evaluation Pipeline
💡 Novelty → ⚙️ Feasibility → 🌱 Sustainability → 📈 Impact → 👤 UX → 🚀 Future Scope
01
💡

Novelty

How fresh and groundbreaking is the idea? Does it introduce a new perspective or creatively reimagine an existing solution?

Originality & Creativity
02
⚙️

Complexity

Does the solution tackle a genuinely hard problem? The depth of technical and domain complexity is evaluated as a strength.

Technical Depth
03
🎯

Clarity

Is the problem statement and proposed solution communicated in a clear, structured, and compelling way to a non-expert audience?

Communication Quality
04
✅

Feasibility

Can this idea realistically be built with available resources, existing technology, and within a reasonable timeline by the team?

Technical Viability
05
🔧

Practicability

Can the prototype be deployed and used effectively in the real world, beyond a controlled lab environment? Is it actually usable?

Real-World Deployment
06
🌱

Sustainability

Does the solution account for long-term viability — environmental responsibility, economic efficiency, and self-sustaining operations?

Long-Term Viability
07
📈

Scale of Impact

How many people, communities, or sectors does this solution benefit? Judges weigh breadth and depth of potential societal transformation.

Societal Reach
08
👤

User Experience

Is the interface intuitive and accessible? Does the prototype provide a seamless, delightful experience for the end user?

Design & Usability
09
🚀

Future Scope

How extensible is the solution? Does it open new avenues for development, scaling, or pivoting into adjacent use cases?

Scalability & Growth
🏆
Score high on all 9 criteria to maximize your chances of qualifying for the SIH National Round nomination from DYPSEM Kolhapur.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the India Smart Hackathon SIH participation and rules.

No, individual registrations are strictly not allowed. Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is exclusively a collaborative, team-based hackathon. You must form a complete squad of 6 members and register through your designated Team Leader using the official Google Form.

Each squad must consist of exactly 6 members (1 Team Leader + 5 members). Furthermore, in accordance with official Smart India Hackathon guidelines, having at least one female participant is strongly required to ensure gender diversity and eligibility for national SIH qualification.

No, cross-college or inter-institute teams are not permitted. For this internal hackathon, all 6 team members must be enrolled students of D Y Patil School of Engineering and Management (DYPSEM Kolhapur). Every team must also submit an institutional College Authorization Letter verified by college authorities.

Each team can submit a proposal for only 1 Problem Statement (or 1 Student Innovation idea under Open Innovation). We strongly advise squads to select the challenge aligning closely with their core technical skills and focus on delivering a high-quality Idea PPT and architectural prototype.

⏰ 11 September 2026 is the critical deadline! All team registrations, member details, college authorization letters, and Stage 02 Idea PPT submissions must be submitted and verified before this date. Late submissions will not be considered for jury evaluation.

Yes! Both Software edition and Hardware edition problem statements from official SIH tracks are eligible. Lab facilities, internet access, power supply, and hardware testing benches will be facilitated on campus during the internal hackathon.
