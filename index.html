Milestones

Event Roadmap & Schedule

Key stages from team registration to the grand internal hackathon finale.

Stage 01 • Active Now Registrations Open

Team Formation & Google Form Registration

Form a team of up to 6 members (with recommended female participation as per SIH guidelines) and complete registration via the official Google Form link.

Stage 02 Phase II

Idea PPT Submission & Shortlisting

Submit your idea presentation detailing problem background, proposed tech stack, architecture diagram, and expected societal impact.

Stage 03 • Grand Finale 11 September

Internal Hackathon & Live Pitch (11 September)

Shortlisted teams will compete in the campus-wide hackathon at D Y Patil School of Engineering and Management on 11 September with continuous evaluation and jury rounds.

Stage 04 National Round

SIH National Nomination & Incubation

Top winning teams will be officially nominated to represent the college at the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) national portal with faculty and industry mentors.
